Everything around the software: installation, the trial, the license, the account that carries your settings from one computer to another, and updates.

Installing MyStrow

Download MyStrow from the download page.

  • Windows 10 / 11: run the installer. MyStrow is digitally signed. If an antivirus still blocks it, it's a false alarm: restore it and add an exception.
  • Mac: choose the right version, Apple Silicon (M1 to M5, macOS 13 Ventura or later) or Intel (macOS 11 Big Sur or later), open the DMG and drag MyStrow into Applications.

At startup, the splash screen checks the License, the DMX Output and the Controller. On first launch on Windows, allow MyStrow through the firewall: it's needed for Art-Net nodes and the tablet.

The free trial and the plans

MyStrow can be tried free for 15 days, with no credit card, with every feature, DMX output included. After that, three plans: Monthly (no commitment), Annual and Lifetime (one-off payment, lifetime updates). Current prices: Shop.

Payment goes through Stripe. Your account is created automatically at payment, and your credentials arrive by email at the address used for the purchase (check your spam).

The MyStrow Pack (USB-DMX interface + license) includes its license: plug in the interface and click Activate the connected MyStrow interface in My account, or follow the mystrow.fr/activate page.

Signing in

The login window accepts:

  • the email and password received after purchase;
  • Continue with Google or Continue with Apple: sign-in finishes in the browser.

Forgot password?: enter the account email and your credentials are sent again (check spam too).

Two devices per license

A license can be active on 2 devices at once (for example the front-of-house PC and a laptop). To use a third, free one up: 🖥 Free up a device in my account, or sign out on the old one (My account › Sign out).

My account (menu About › 🔑 License) shows the status (Active license, trial, expired), the devices, and gives access to ⚙ Manage my subscription (payment method, invoices, cancellation) and Renew / Change plan.

Playing without internet

The lights never need internet. The license is checked when the computer is online, then MyStrow works for up to 16 days without a connection. Go online from time to time (for example before a tour) to reset the counter.

A “Network error” message at startup only means the server couldn't be reached: check the connection, the show isn't at risk.

Your account carries your settings

When signed in, MyStrow automatically sends your patch, effects, memories and controller pages to your account. On another computer, sign in: they're fetched at startup, no button to press.

If the computer already has its own settings, different from the account's, MyStrow asks once which ones to keep. Offline, nothing blocks: changes are sent when the network comes back.

Shows, files and configuration

A show is a .tui file containing the playlist, its REC Light sequences, the patch and the settings. File menu: 📄 New Show, 📂 Open Show, 💾 Save Show, 💾 Save As, 📋 Recent.

  • 📤 Export a configuration / 📥 Import a configuration: back up or transfer the controller configuration (memories, pages, assigned effects).
  • 🗑️ Clear data: empty memories or effects to start from scratch.

Keep a copy of your shows and audio files on a USB stick: a show points to its media, which must stay in the same place.

Updates and language

MyStrow checks for updates at startup. About › ℹ️ Updates lets you check by hand (⟳ Recheck) and install (Install →). If the show has unsaved changes, MyStrow offers Save and install. What's new in each version: 🆕 What's new? or the What's new page.

If an update doesn't go through, download the latest version from the website and install it over the top: shows and settings are kept.

🌐 Language / Langue: French, English, Spanish, German, Portuguese, Italian and Japanese. The About menu also has 💡 Submit an idea and 🔄 Restart.

Frequently asked questions

I didn't receive my credentials after purchase.

Check spam and Gmail's Promotions tab, and check the address typed at checkout (one typo and the email goes nowhere). Then use “Forgot password?” in the login window, or write to us.

“2 devices maximum allowed for this account”.

The license is already active on two computers. Free one up from 🖥 Free up a device in my account, or sign out on the old computer.

My trial is over, do I lose my shows?

No. Your shows, patch and settings stay on the computer and in your account. Get a license and everything is there.

On Mac, MyStrow won't open.

Check your macOS version (Apple Silicon: macOS 13 minimum; Intel: macOS 11 minimum) and download the right version again. If MyStrow closes about thirty seconds after opening, an antivirus is often the cause.
