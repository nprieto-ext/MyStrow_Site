Introduction

From manual to automatic: light sequences

Controlling your lights live is good. Programming them so they run exactly the same way at every performance is even better. MyStrow offers two complementary approaches: spontaneous recording with Light Rec, and structured programming on the timeline.

Whichever method you choose, the result is the same: precise sequences, synced to your music, endlessly repeatable with no extra effort on show night.

Available approaches

Two ways to build your sequences

MyStrow offers two sequencing modes, suited to different workflows. Both can coexist in the same show.

Approach 1

🔴 Light Rec — Live recording

You perform your show live while MyStrow records everything. Every color, every level change, every cue point is captured automatically and turned into clips on the timeline.

Approach 2

🎬 Timeline — Precise programming

You draw your sequences straight onto the timeline by placing color clips on a time grid. Ideal for repeat shows where every second has to be identical.

Method 1 — Light Rec

Recording your show live

1

Start the music and enable Light Rec

In the MyStrow playlist, select the track you want to build your sequence on. Press Play to start, then click the REC button in the light bar. MyStrow starts recording every move you make on the interface or on the AKAI APC mini.

2

Perform your show in real time

Everything you do while recording is captured: color changes on groups, faders going up and down, mode switches. Play freely — you are building your sequence without even realising it.

3

Stop the recording

Click the REC button again to stop. MyStrow automatically turns all your actions into colored clips on the track's timeline. Each clip represents a light state at a precise moment.

4

Edit and fine-tune the clips

Open the timeline editor (the Edit button on the track). You can move, resize, cut or delete each clip. Adjust color, intensity and transitions. Precision is absolute — down to the millisecond.

5

Switch to Light Play mode and replay

With your sequence ready, change the track's mode to Light Play. From now on, every time the track plays, MyStrow automatically replays your recorded sequence — identical, precise, with nothing for you to do.

Method 2 — Timeline

Programming your sequence on the timeline

1

Open the timeline editor

In the playlist, click the Edit button on a track. The timeline editor opens with a time grid where each row represents a fixture group (Front, Top 1, Top 2…).

2

Create color clips

Click and drag on a track to create a clip. Choose the color, the intensity and, if you like, a secondary color (bi-color). Clips snap together and overlap freely on each group track.

3

Sync with the music

Play the music from inside the editor and adjust your clips in real time. The track's waveform is displayed below the timeline, letting you align your light changes with accents, drops or choruses.

4

Save and attach to the track

Your sequence is automatically linked to the track inside the show file (.tui). Next time you open the show, everything is there — sequence, colors, timings. Reload, press Play, and off you go.

Frequently asked questions

Light sequences: your questions

Which method should I choose: Light Rec or the timeline?

Light Rec is ideal if you have musical instinct and like to improvise. The timeline suits you better if you want absolute precision or highly structured shows. In practice you often use both: record with Rec, then fine-tune on the timeline.

Can I edit a sequence after recording it?

Yes, completely. Open the timeline editor and you will find all your recorded clips. You can move them, resize them, change their color or intensity, add new ones or delete them.

Does Light Play mode work with every fixture?

Yes. MyStrow sends the sequences to all your patched fixtures, whatever their DMX profile (3CH, 5CH, RGBW, moving head…). Color conversions are handled automatically by the DMX engine.

How many tracks can I program in one show?

There is no limit. Every playlist entry can have its own independent light sequence. A 30-track show can have 30 different sequences, all saved in the same .tui file.
