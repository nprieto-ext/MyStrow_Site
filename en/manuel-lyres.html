Moving heads:
positions and movement

Moving heads move, change colour and gobo. This chapter gathers everything about them: settings, movement, stored positions and motion effects.

First: set up the patch properly

Before moving a head, four patch settings (Edit › 🔌 DMX Patch, the head's sheet) avoid most surprises:

  • the DMX mode identical to the head's;
  • the Movement limits, so it never blinds the audience or aims at a wall;
  • Pan inverted / Tilt inverted if the head is hung upside down;
  • calibrating the colour wheel and the gobo wheel.

Details: Patch chapter.

Moving heads from the lighting plan

  1. Select one or more moving heads on the lighting plan.
  2. In the head's panel, drag the position point. Mouse wheel: Tilt; Ctrl + wheel: Pan; double-click: back to the centre (⊕ Centre).
  3. Aim mode: click a point on the stage, all selected heads point at it.

⇄ SYM turns on Pan symmetry: heads to the right of the selection's axis move with inverted Pan, those on the left with normal Pan. A movement becomes a stage-left / stage-right mirror.

Position presets

The panel's Presets section recalls positions in one click. The built-in presets are Centre, Front, Floor, Ceiling, Left and Right. Add your own with the save button (“save the current position as a new preset”), for example “Singer”, “Drums”, “Audience”.

To play them from the pad surface or the controller, record them on POS pads: 🔴 REC Memory, then a POS pad. See the Live playing chapter.

Movement: quick effects and effects

  • Auto effects in the head's panel: Circle, Figure 8, Horizontal sweep, Vertical sweep, Random, with Speed and Amplitude. Clicking the running effect stops it.
  • QUICK EFFECTS on the selection: Circle, Figure 8, Pan sweep, Tilt sweep, with SPEED, AMPLITUDE and PHASE OFFSET (0 = all together, 100 = wave).
  • For a saved, replayable movement (on an E button, an FX pad, a memory or REC Light): a Pan/Tilt layer in the effect editor, with its Circle, Eight, Infinity, Swing, Pendulum and Square shapes, a POSITION to revolve around and SYM.
  • In Live mode, the MOVEMENT tab lets the AI choose the figures. See the AI and Live chapter.

Driving moving heads with a game controller

Connection › 🎮 Control › 🎮 Gamepad: a console controller (Xbox, PlayStation…) connected over USB or Bluetooth drives the moving heads.

  • Sticks (pan/tilt) tab: the left stick moves the heads, the right one fine-tunes. The further you push, the faster they go; let go and they stop there. Set the Dead zone, the speed (Full sweep in), the Target (lighting plan selection or all moving heads) and Pan / Tilt inversion, then tick Enable gamepad control.
  • Buttons tab: each button can trigger a memory, an effect, an FX pad or control the sequencer. Two pages, the second while holding L2.

A DualShock 3 on Windows needs a dedicated driver (DsHidMini). Full guide: driving moving heads with a game controller.

Frequently asked questions

My moving head goes all over the place.

The patch's DMX mode doesn't match the head's, and the channels are shifted. Choose the same mode in the patch and check the address.

My moving head's colours don't match.

On a colour-wheel head, calibrate the wheel in the patch (🎨 Calibrate the color wheel), then copy the calibration to heads of the same model.

A circle looks like an oval or a figure eight.

Enter the head's mechanical Pan/Tilt range in the patch (540°/270° on most, see its manual): it's used to give movements the right shape.

How do I make a stage-left / stage-right mirror?

Turn on ⇄ SYM on the lighting plan, or the SYM column (Pan inverted) in a Pan/Tilt layer of the effect editor.
