Example

What it looks like

A real question we are often asked, and the answer the AI gives once the manual is attached:

My fixture changes colour on its own, even though I haven't started anything. Why?
It's almost always a different DMX mode between the fixture and the patch: the channels are shifted and one of them lands on "auto" or "sound".

1. Check the mode shown in the fixture's menu (often "7CH", "d7", "CH 9").
2. In the DMX patch, choose exactly the same mode.
3. Make sure the patch address and the fixture address are the same. The easiest way: ✏️ Edit › ⚡ Auto address, then copy the addresses onto the fixtures.
And to check channel by channel?
In the main window, open Edit › 🔬 DMX Tester: raise each channel one by one and see which one does what on the fixture.

More question ideas: "I have 4 PARs and 2 moving heads, where do I start?", "How do I sync my lights to a playlist?", "My USB-DMX interface isn't detected, what should I check?", "Make me a checklist before a wedding".

Going further

Keep it at hand

Attaching the file to each new conversation works everywhere. If you use it often, store it once and for all:

📁 ChatGPT and Claude Projects

Create a "MyStrow" project, add the file to its files and the prompt to its instructions. Every conversation in the project then knows the manual, with nothing to attach. (Depending on your plan.)

📓 NotebookLM (Google)

Free, and built for this: add the file as a source, and every answer points to the exact passage of the manual. Ideal for checking what the AI says.

Good to know

The limits, honestly

  • The file follows the latest released version. After a MyStrow update, download it again so the AI knows what's new.
  • The AI can still get it wrong. Much less than blindfolded, but if a menu it mentions doesn't show up on your screen, ask it for the passage of the manual that describes it, or check the online manual.
  • Free accounts have quotas. The number of attachments per day is limited depending on the AI and the plan. One file is enough for a whole conversation.
  • It explains, it doesn't control. The manual teaches the AI to guide you, not to switch on your fixtures.

The AI can't find the answer? Maybe it's missing from the manual. Write to us via the contact page: we'll answer you, and complete the documentation for the next person.
