MyStrow Manual

Every feature of the software, chapter by chapter, with the exact menus. Looking for a quick answer? Start with the help page.

By MyStrow·Updated 18 November 2026
Quick help ›Download MyStrow ↓
Chapters
Chapter 1

🌐 DMX output

Choosing and connecting a USB-DMX interface or an Art-Net node, setting up the network, handling up to 4 universes, testing the output and diagnosing a silent output.

Chapter 2

🔌 Patch and fixtures

Adding fixtures from the 5,000+ profile library, choosing the DMX mode, setting addresses, universes and groups, importing a patch, creating a missing fixture and setting up moving heads, strobe and wheels.

Chapter 3

🔑 Installation, license and account

Installing on Windows or Mac, 15-day free trial, signing in (email, Google, Apple), license on 2 devices, offline use, account sync, updates, shows and language.

Chapter 4

🗺️ 2D and 3D lighting plan

The 2D lighting plan (selection, per-fixture settings, selection groups, CLEAR, DMX cut) and the 3D visualisation (sets, trusses, fixture positioning, GLB scene import, video screens).

Chapter 5

🎹 Live playing

Playing light live with the pad grid and faders, recording and recalling memories, chaining cues, using FLASH, FLASH KILL, GO and TAP, effect and position pads, and pages.

Chapter 6

🎯 Moving heads and movement

Driving moving heads. Patch settings, moving them on the lighting plan, position presets, aim mode, symmetry, movement effects, POS positions and game controller.

Chapter 7

🎨 Effect editor

Create an effect: layers, shapes, speed, offset, assignment to buttons E1 to E8, recipes.

Chapter 8

🎬 Playlist and REC Light

The playlist (audio, video, images, pauses, web pages), each media item's light mode, the REC Light editor (tracks, colour blocks, memories, effects, positions, sequences), AI generation, cartridges and scheduled start.

Chapter 9

💡 AI Light and Live mode

Making fixtures react to music without programming. AI Light per playlist item, Live mode on any source (Spotify, DJ software, microphone, mixing desk), MUSICAL AI and MOOD AI modes, tabs and settings.

Chapter 10

🎛️ Controllers and shortcuts

Connecting an AKAI APC mini or a Launchpad, getting any MIDI or DJ controller recognised with the wizard, driving MyStrow from a Stream Deck or a lighting console (DMX input), and customising keyboard shortcuts.

Chapter 11

📱 Tablet, OBS, vMix and AI assistant

Controlling MyStrow from a tablet or phone, the standalone tablet app, triggering lights from OBS or vMix, and commanding MyStrow by talking to Claude.

Chapter 12

🩺 Troubleshooting

What to do when nothing lights up, when a fixture moves on its own, when strobe, the tablet, the Mac, an antivirus, the license or an update cause problems. Diagnostic tools and how to contact us.

The manual follows MyStrow's releases. Something missing? Tell us and we'll add it.
