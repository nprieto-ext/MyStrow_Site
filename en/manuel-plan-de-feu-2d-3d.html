Lighting plan
in 2D and 3D

The lighting plan shows your fixtures where they are on stage, and what they're doing in real time. You use it to select and adjust fixtures by hand, and to prepare a show without any hardware thanks to the 3D view.

The 2D lighting plan

In the main window, the lighting plan shows each patched fixture with its live colour and intensity, the front at the bottom (FRONT / BOTTOM) and the back at the top (BACK / TOP).

  • Mouse wheel: zoom; middle click or Alt + drag: move the view; 0: whole plan.
  • The layout (move, align, spread) is edited in Edit › 🔌 DMX Patch, 🎭 Lighting plan tab: drag to move, Shift + drag to snap, align and spread to tidy a row. See the Patch chapter.

Selecting and adjusting fixtures by hand

  1. Click a fixture (or draw a rectangle, Ctrl+A for all). ◀ Prev / Next ▶ (or the keyboard arrows) step through the selection fixture by fixture.
  2. The settings panel opens: COLOUR, Dim, Strobe, and depending on the device colour wheel, gobo, rotation, prism, SPECIAL CHANNELS and PRESETS.
  3. 🎚 Sliders switches to raw view: one slider per DMX channel, following the output live.

Quick selection groups: select fixtures, then ➕ Add a group from selection (for example “Cyc”, “FOH”). They're recalled in one click; ⚙ Manage groups to rename or reorder them.

For LED bars and matrices, the BAR / MATRIX EFFECTS section offers Chase, Wave, Scrolling rainbow and Sparkle, with a direction (horizontal, vertical, diagonal, from the centre).

CLEAR and cutting the DMX output

  • Clear: turns off all the fixtures on the lighting plan and releases whatever was set by hand.
  • The Enable / disable DMX output button stops sending DMX (MyStrow asks for confirmation). Handy for preparing a show without the fixtures reacting.

The 3D visualisation

View › 3D plan, or the Show the lighting plan in 3D button, opens a 3D window that replays the show in real time, beams included. You can prepare a whole show at home, with no fixtures.

  • Camera: drag to orbit, mouse wheel to zoom, double-click to reset; FRONT, TOP, SIDE views.
  • Room ambience: the ambient light, from pitch dark to daylight.
  • Render quality (Low, Medium, High): lower it if the display stutters with many fixtures.
  • Keep the window on top: to leave it on a second screen.

Sets, trusses and imported scene

The SET menu offers ready-made scenes: concert stage, 6×5 m stage, outdoor festival, nightclub, mobile DJ, conference, Christmas tree, or no set.

  • Truss editor: + Add a truss, then set its height, front/back position and left and right edges. Changes apply live.
  • IMPORT SCENE: load your own venue as .glb or .gltf (exportable from Blender, SketchUp or Vectorworks). An AI can also build it: creating your 3D set with AI.
  • VIDEO SCREENS: add screens to the set; with Video feed, they show the playlist's current media.

Placing fixtures in 3D

Fixture positioning lists each device with its position: X (left/right), Z (front/back), Y (hanging height), and its angles: RX (0° = hung from the ceiling, 180° = standing on the floor), RY (horizontal orientation of the base), RZ (roll).

  • Tick several rows to set them together; Spread places them at regular intervals between two bounds.
  • ↕ Flip (0° ↔ 180°): move a device from the ceiling to the floor.
  • Beam, Angle, Size: beam power and spread, body size in 3D. These settings only change the display, never the DMX output.
Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare a show without fixtures?

Yes. Patch your fixtures, open the 3D view (View › 3D plan): everything you play, memories, effects, REC Light, is shown in real time. On the day, just plug in the DMX interface.

The 3D view stutters.

Lower the Render quality (Low or Medium) and close other heavy programs. The volumetric beam detail is what costs the most.

My moving heads point the wrong way in 3D.

Check their hanging in Fixture positioning (RX = 0° on the ceiling, 180° on the floor, ↕ Flip to switch) and the RY orientation. On the DMX side, inverted Pan/Tilt is set in the patch.
