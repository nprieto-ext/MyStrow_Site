Other compatible controllers

Other MIDI control surfaces work with MyStrow through remapping. They are compatible, but without the native plug & play mapping of the APC mini mk2.

⭐ AKAI APC mini mk2 AKAI APC mini mk1 AKAI APC40 AKAI MIDImix Novation Launchpad Mini MK1 Novation Launchpad Mini MK2 ⭐ Novation Launchpad Mini MK3
No controller? No problem.

MyStrow includes an on-screen AKAI simulator — an 8×8 pad grid and 9 faders, usable with the mouse or on a touchscreen. You can also drive everything from keyboard and mouse. The physical controller gives the best live experience, but stays entirely optional.
